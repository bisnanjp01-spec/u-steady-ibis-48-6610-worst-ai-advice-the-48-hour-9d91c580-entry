One line of the worst possible advice an AI could give, in 140 characters or fewer: a confident, tidy assistant voice tells you to microwave your phone for 30 seconds to recharge it, because the heat tops up the lithium cells. The program writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt`, where the house judge compares it two at a time against the other entries on funniness, tightness, AI voice, and concreteness.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
